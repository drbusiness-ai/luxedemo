What each fee is

No surprises at the
trustee office.

The fee model used above. Rates change, so we confirm each one on the day you transact.

DLD transfer fee, 4% of the price +

Paid to the Dubai Land Department when ownership transfers. On a ready property it is paid at the trustee office on transfer day; on off-plan it is usually paid when the sale is registered in Oqood. Some developer launches cover part of it.

DLD admin fee and trustee office fee +

The admin fee covers issuing the title deed (AED 580 in this model). Registration trustee offices charge AED 4,000 + 5% VAT for properties of AED 500,000 and above, or AED 2,000 + VAT below that.

Agency commission, 2% + VAT +

The market norm on resale purchases in Dubai. On many off-plan launches the developer pays the agency, so the buyer pays nothing. We always show you which applies before you sign.

Mortgage costs +

Mortgage registration with the DLD is 0.25% of the loan plus AED 290. Banks typically charge an arrangement fee of up to 1% of the loan and a valuation fee of around AED 3,000, both plus 5% VAT, which the calculator includes.

Why net yield matters more than gross +

Gross yield is rent divided by price. Net yield subtracts the service charge, maintenance and management, and divides by everything you actually paid, including purchase costs. It is the fair way to compare two properties.

The Golden Visa property route +

Owning property worth AED 2M or more can support an application for the 10-year Golden Visa. Conditions apply to mortgaged and off-plan property and to joint ownership, and the final decision rests with the authorities. We walk you through the documents.
