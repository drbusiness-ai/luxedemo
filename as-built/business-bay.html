Questions buyers ask

Business Bay, answered.

Can foreigners buy in Business Bay? +

Yes. Business Bay is a designated freehold area, so international buyers can own property outright, with the title deed registered at the Dubai Land Department.

How do I check a building's service charge? +

Approved service charges are published through the Dubai Land Department's service charge index, viewable in the Dubai REST app. We pull the figure for every building on your shortlist and show it in your net yield.

Can I rent my apartment short-term? +

Holiday home rentals need a permit from Dubai's tourism authority and must be allowed by the building. Some towers welcome them, others restrict them, so we confirm before you buy if this is your plan.

Does buying here qualify me for a Golden Visa? +

Property worth AED 2M or more, in Business Bay or elsewhere in Dubai, can support a 10-year Golden Visa application. Check your scenario in the True Cost Calculator.

Canal view or city view? +

Canal and Burj views usually command higher prices and rents, but not always higher net yields. We compare achieved rents for both in the same building before you decide.
