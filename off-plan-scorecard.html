Off-plan Due Diligence Scorecard

Score the project before you pay the deposit.

Off-plan can be the smartest way into Dubai, or the most stressful. Six checks, weighted and scored out of 100, tell you which one you are looking at, in plain language.

Request a scorecard See a sample

What we score

Six checks, from
official sources.

Each check is scored against public registers and transaction data wherever they exist. Where judgement is involved, we show our reasoning.

0125 pts

Developer delivery track record

Completed projects, how late each handover was against the promise, and snagging history.

Source DLD project and completion records

0220 pts

Escrow & RERA registration

Is the project registered with RERA, paid into a dedicated escrow account, on land the developer owns?

Source RERA project and escrow registers

0315 pts

Payment plan maths

Cash needed before keys, the handover lump sum, and whether a mortgage can realistically cover it.

Source Developer schedule, modelled by us

0415 pts

Construction progress

Percentage complete against the timeline, from the latest official progress update and site visits.

Source DLD progress updates, site visits

0515 pts

Resale premium

What the same developer's earlier launches resell for today, compared with their launch prices.

Source DLD transaction data

0610 pts

Service charge history

What the developer's completed buildings charge per sq ft, and how that has moved year to year.

Source DLD service charge index
